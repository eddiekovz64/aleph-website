## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences, weighted equally:
- **The student:** a young Jewish man (college-age or young professional), often with little background in learning, deciding whether to spend a few weeks in Jerusalem.
- **The referring rabbi:** campus and outreach rabbis (e.g. Meor, Olami) checking that Aleph is serious and safe to send students to.

The student decides; the rabbi vouches. The site has to convince both.

## Product Purpose
Aleph is a short, immersive bridge yeshiva program in Jerusalem. A small group of high-caliber students spends a few focused weeks learning Torah, growing personally, traveling Israel and training physically, then can stay on for Remote Work Study. Success: qualified young men apply, and referring rabbis trust Aleph with their students.

## Positioning
Aleph is run by young, talented, next-generation rabbis (Rabbi Avi Kovel, Director; Rabbi Jimmy Feterman, Assistant Director). They run small trips of high-caliber students and tailor each trip to meet every student where he is, pushing each one to grow to his maximum potential in a short time. Aleph is cooler and more current than typical kiruv programs: fitness, remote work, business mentors and travel are part of the program, not extras.

## Operating Context
- Two winter programs: Yeshiva Experience (Dec 20 – Jan 3, $299) and Remote Work Study (Jan 4 – 31, up to 4 weeks, $499). Summer 2027: two-week Yeshiva Experience starting late May / early June, then Remote Work Study for a month or more.
- Applying: Google Form (https://forms.gle/NFYhvbsHQXUxLLTcA), then interview, deposit, flights, arrival.
- Visitors arrive from rabbi referrals, shared links (social preview image), and the trip-listing site thumbnail.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS in `site/`, deployed to GitHub Pages at alephexperience.com.
- The video is self-hosted (`site/video/aleph.mp4`), not on YouTube.
- Terminology: Gemara, Chumash with Rashi, Ta'amei HaMitzvos, Mussar, Tefilla, Shabbos/Shabbat, kiruv, shmiras hamitzvos.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "Aleph"; gold Aleph logo (`site/images/logo-gold.png`).
- Palette and fonts are shared with the flyers, slide deck and lower-thirds: navy #0A1628, gold #C9A84C, cream #F5F0E8; Cormorant Garamond, Crimson Pro, Inter.
- Voice: cool and current, not stuffy or institutional. Confident about wanting serious students.
- The rabbis' quotes are final, approved wording and must not be edited.

## Product Principles
1. Personal over institutional: two young rabbis and a small group, not a big yeshiva.
2. High bar, warm hand: for serious young men, and every student starts at his own level.
3. The whole person: learning, growth, travel, fitness and career belong together.
4. Show real people and real places; never stock photos or invented proof.
